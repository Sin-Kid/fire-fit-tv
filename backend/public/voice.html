FitCoach Voice Remote

Your TV has no microphone for apps, so this page listens for it. Say a command and the TV does it.

Not connected

Allow the microphone when the browser asks. Works in Chrome, Edge and Safari.

  • “Skip” or “next”
  • “Wait” / “pause”, then “go” / “resume”
  • “More rest” or “increase rest” (+15 s), “less rest” (−15 s)
  • “Easier” / “make it easy”, “normal” to switch back
  • “Mute” / “unmute”, “end workout”
